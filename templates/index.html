Conversational AI Evaluation Wizard

From Metrics to Meaningful Insights

📌 About the Project

This project is part of a master's thesis titled “A Comparative Study of Evaluation Metrics for Conversational AI: Toward a Unified Framework”. The tool aims to bridge the disconnect between traditional automatic evaluation metrics like BLEU, ROUGE, and METEOR, and real human judgment when assessing AI-generated conversational responses.

To address this, we introduce a composite scoring framework that intelligently blends these traditional metrics with human feedback to deliver a more reliable, semantically-aware, and human-aligned evaluation.

🎯 Why Was This Needed?

  • BLEU penalizes paraphrasing and neglects meaning.
  • ROUGE focuses on recall but may ignore fluency.
  • METEOR captures synonyms but remains surface-level.
  • Human feedback is essential, but not scalable on its own.

The proposed Composite Score merges automated scores and human ratings using configurable weights (α and β), ensuring outputs are not just linguistically similar — but also contextually and semantically appropriate.

🔍 What the App Does

  1. Step 1: Upload your dataset or generate a sample file.
  2. Step 2: Preview and validate the reference-response pairs.
  3. Step 3: Compute BLEU, ROUGE-L, METEOR, and Composite Scores.
  4. Step 4: Explore intuitive visualizations (averages, correlations, PR curves, and more).

This step-by-step wizard helps developers, researchers, and linguists assess the quality of AI-generated conversations from both technical and human-centric perspectives.

📊 Visual Dashboard Highlights

  • 📊 Average Scores: See how each metric behaves across the dataset.
  • 📈 Correlation: Check how closely each metric reflects human scores.
  • 📉 Score Variability: Measure consistency across responses.
  • 🎯 Precision-Recall: Detect a metric’s ability to catch high-quality outputs.
  • 📐 Delta Histogram: Understand how much the composite improves over traditional scores.

📘 Research Contribution

This project presents a practical implementation of the research thesis: “A Comparative Study of Evaluation Metrics for Conversational AI: Toward a Unified Framework”.

It contributes a scalable, explainable, and extensible framework that unifies automated evaluation with human-aligned reasoning. It also lays the foundation for future integration of advanced techniques like BERTScore, RLHF, and context-awareness evaluation.

As a dual-purpose toolkit, it serves both academic validation and practical auditing of dialogue systems.
